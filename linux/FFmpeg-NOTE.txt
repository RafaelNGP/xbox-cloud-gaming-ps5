FFmpeg in psbox
===============

psbox links a slice of FFmpeg n7.1.2 statically: libavcodec, libswresample
and libavutil, with the H.264 and Opus decoders, the Opus encoder and the
H.264 / Opus parsers only. FFmpeg is licensed under the GNU Lesser General
Public License version 2.1 or later (FFmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt); it was built
without --enable-gpl, --enable-version3 or --enable-nonfree.

Configure flags (tools/build-ffmpeg.sh, "host"):

    --enable-static --disable-shared --enable-pic
    --disable-programs --disable-doc --disable-network --disable-autodetect
    --disable-everything --disable-avdevice --disable-avformat --disable-avfilter
    --disable-swscale --disable-postproc
    --enable-decoder=h264,opus --enable-encoder=opus --enable-parser=h264,opus
    --disable-debug --cc=clang

Source: https://ffmpeg.org (tag n7.1.2), also the deps/ffmpeg submodule of
https://github.com/RafaelNGP/xbox-cloud-gaming-ps5 at this release's tag.
To relink psbox with another FFmpeg build, build the project from that
source (README.md) with your FFmpeg in place of deps/ffmpeg: every object
psbox is made of is built from the same repository.
